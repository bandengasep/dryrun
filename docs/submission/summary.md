DryRun turns a job description and a resume into the interview you're actually about to have. It diffs the two documents into evidenced gaps — every gap citing the JD line that demands it and the resume line that's silent — compiles the questions those gaps predict, rehearses them live against an AI interviewer, and debriefs you by quoting your own answers back. It's for job-seekers who want prep they can audit: nothing is displayed without a mechanically validated receipt, and nothing is graded — no scores, just your own words and the evidence. Live at https://dryrun-web-pi.vercel.app — no account needed; /compile?mock=1 runs the whole journey with zero API keys.
